About Urban Leather Co.

Founded in 2024 in Austin, Texas, Urban Leather Co. is an independent leathercraft workshop dedicated to creating rugged, refined goods built for a lifetime of adventure. Our workshop combines traditional saddle-making methods with contemporary functional design.

Full-Grain Leather & Vegetable Tanning

We source only the top layer of cowhide, known as full-grain leather. Unlike corrected or bonded leather, full-grain retains natural markings, strength, and develops a rich, golden patina over years of use. All our leather is vegetable-tanned using organic tree barks and natural tannins, free of toxic chrome salts.

Hand-Stitched Durability

Every stress point across our bags, belts, and wallets is reinforced with double-needle saddle stitching using bonded nylon thread. If a machine stitch breaks, the entire seam can unravel; our traditional hand stitching ensures that even if one loop experiences heavy wear, the rest of the seam remains completely intact.

Our Lifetime Craftsmanship Guarantee

We stand unconditionally behind every product we make. All solid brass hardware, buckles, rivets, and stitching are guaranteed for life. If any component ever fails under normal use, send it back to our Austin studio and we will repair or replace it free of charge.

Domestic Shipping & Fast Dispatch

All orders are packed and dispatched within 1–2 business days directly from our Austin studio via USPS Priority Mail and UPS Ground. Every order over $100 qualifies for free nationwide standard shipping with full door-to-door tracking.
