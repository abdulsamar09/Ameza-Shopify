Returns & Refunds Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • AMEZA Marketplace Inc. • Austin, Texas

1. 30-Day Customer Satisfaction Guarantee

We want you to love your purchases. If you are not completely satisfied with your order, AMEZA provides a straightforward 30-day return policy from the confirmed date of carrier delivery for all eligible products.

2. Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a refund, returned items must satisfy the following conditions:

  • Must be initiated within 30 calendar days of confirmed delivery.
  • Must be unused, unwashed, and in the original condition received, with all manufacturer tags, authentication cards, and original packaging intact.
  • Electronics and smartwatches must be factory-reset and clear of personal accounts or locks.

Non-returnable items include personalized/custom-engraved products, final sale clearance items, and opened intimate hygiene products.

3. How to Initiate a Return

  1. Log into your account and navigate to Orders & Tracking.
  2. Select the specific sub-order or item you wish to return and click "Request Return".
  3. Print the prepaid domestic return shipping label provided by the merchant or AMEZA support.
  4. Drop off the package at any authorized USPS, UPS, or FedEx location.

4. Inspection & Refund Processing

Upon receipt, the seller inspects the returned merchandise within 2 business days. Once approved, the refund is automatically disbursed to your original payment method (Credit card, PayPal, or Apple Pay). Depending on your banking institution, funds typically reflect in your account within 3 to 5 business days.

5. Restocking & Return Shipping Fees

If an item is returned due to seller error, shipping damage, or defective quality, return shipping is 100% free. For discretionary returns (e.g., buyer remorse or incorrect sizing), a flat $5.99 return postage fee may be deducted from your refund amount. AMEZA never charges hidden restocking fees.
