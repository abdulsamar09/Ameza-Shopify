Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • AMEZA Marketplace Inc. • Austin, Texas

1. Domestic U.S. Shipping Overview

AMEZA Marketplace partners with premier domestic carriers including the United States Postal Service (USPS), United Parcel Service (UPS), and FedEx to guarantee safe, timely, and trackable delivery to all 50 U.S. states and the District of Columbia.

2. Shipping Speeds & Pricing Tiers

Service Tier Estimated Transit Time Cost
Standard Shipping (USPS Ground Advantage) 3 – 5 Business Days $5.99 (FREE on orders over $100.00)
Express Priority (USPS Priority / UPS 2nd Day) 2 – 3 Business Days $12.99
Overnight Expedited (FedEx / UPS Next Day) 1 Business Day $24.99

3. Order Processing & Multi-Vendor Shipments

Orders are dispatched by individual merchants within 1 to 2 business days of order confirmation (Monday through Friday, excluding federal U.S. holidays). Because AMEZA is a multi-vendor marketplace, items purchased from different independent sellers will be shipped separately from the respective merchant's workshop or warehouse. You will receive unique tracking numbers for each merchant parcel.

4. Tracking Your Order

Once a merchant creates a carrier label, you will receive an email confirmation with your tracking link. You may also track all package milestones at any time in your Orders & Tracking Portal.

5. P.O. Boxes, APO/FPO & U.S. Territories

Standard delivery to U.S. P.O. Boxes, APO/FPO military addresses, Puerto Rico, Guam, and the U.S. Virgin Islands is handled exclusively via USPS. Transit times may take an additional 3 to 7 business days.

6. Damaged or Lost Packages

If your package is damaged during transit or marked delivered but not received, please contact support@ameza.com within 7 business days so our Buyer Protection team can initiate a carrier claim and expedite a replacement or refund.
