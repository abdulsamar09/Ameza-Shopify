Accessibility Statement

Last Updated: October 2026 • AMEZA Marketplace Inc. • Austin, Texas

1. Our Commitment to Accessibility

AMEZA Marketplace Inc. is dedicated to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including individuals with disabilities. We continuously audit and enhance the user experience for everyone, adhering to relevant accessibility standards under Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and the World Wide Web Consortium's Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

2. Accessibility Measures & Features

Throughout our marketplace storefront, account center, and merchant dashboards, we implement key accessibility practices, including:

  • Keyboard Navigation: Fully operable keyboard navigation patterns for all interactive buttons, modals, dropdowns, and form fields.
  • Color Contrast: High-contrast typography and semantic color tokens ensuring readability across dark and light viewing modes.
  • Screen Reader Support: Comprehensive ARIA labels, descriptive alt attributes on catalog imagery, and structured headings.
  • Focus States: Highly visible focus indicators to assist keyboard and assistive technology navigation.

3. Ongoing Evaluation & Feedback

We routinely evaluate our web properties using automated linting tools, assistive screen readers, and manual accessibility audits. Despite our continuous efforts, certain third-party merchant content or embedded resources may occasionally present barriers.
