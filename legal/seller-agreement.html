Seller Agreement & Merchant Terms

Last Updated: October 2026 • AMEZA Marketplace Inc. • Austin, Texas

1. Merchant Engagement & Representation

This Seller Agreement ("Agreement") governs your application, registration, listing, and commercial transactions as an independent merchant on AMEZA Marketplace Inc. By onboarding via our seller portal, submitting products, or accepting customer orders, you agree to comply with this Agreement.

2. Merchant Qualifications & U.S. Tax Compliance (W-9 / 1099-K)

All merchants operating on AMEZA must be legally organized entities or registered sole proprietors in the United States. Sellers must provide a valid Employer Identification Number (EIN) or Social Security Number (SSN) alongside a verified IRS Form W-9 prior to receiving disbursement of funds.

In compliance with U.S. federal law and Internal Revenue Service (IRS) regulations, AMEZA will generate and file annual Form 1099-K reports for gross payments meeting current federal and applicable state reporting thresholds.

3. Marketplace Commission Structure

AMEZA assesses a transparent, performance-based commission on completed product sales:

  • Standard Platform Commission: 10.0% to 15.0% depending on product category, calculated on the gross product sale price.
  • Payment Processing: Standard credit card interchange and merchant gateway fees (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction) are deducted upon checkout settlement.
  • No Hidden Listing Fees: Listing products and managing your digital storefront incurs no monthly recurring platform fee.

4. Order Fulfillment & Dispatch Standards

Merchants are solely responsible for sourcing, packaging, and fulfilling customer orders in compliance with AMEZA quality standards:

  • Orders must be dispatched within 48 hours (2 business days) of placement.
  • Valid tracking numbers from recognized carriers (USPS, UPS, or FedEx) must be uploaded to the order detail interface.
  • Merchants agree to honor customer return requests under the AMEZA 30-Day Customer Satisfaction policy.

5. Holding Period & ACH Disbursements

To safeguard platform trust and protect against disputed transactions or unfulfilled orders, sales proceeds are initially placed in a pending reserve holding period of 7 days following confirmed carrier delivery. Once this hold elapses, funds transition to your Available balance and can be transferred via ACH Direct Deposit to your verified U.S. checking account within 1 to 2 banking business days.

6. Intellectual Property & Product Authenticity

Merchants represent and warrant that all listed goods are genuine, original, and free of counterfeit parts, copyright violations, or trademark infringements. Any seller listing infringing merchandise will face immediate store suspension and forfeiture of pending balances.
