Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • AMEZA Marketplace Inc. • Austin, Texas

1. Overview & Scope

AMEZA Marketplace Inc. ("AMEZA," "we," "us," or "our"), headquartered at 123 Main Street, Austin, TX 78701, is committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, share, and protect information across our website, mobile interfaces, and digital services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, browsing listings, placing orders, registering as a merchant, or contacting customer support:

  • Contact & Identity Data: Full name, email address, postal delivery address, phone number.
  • Financial & Payment Data: Payment card tokenized credentials, billing address, and transaction records handled via PCI-DSS compliant processors.
  • Merchant Verification Data: For sellers, business name, Employer Identification Number (EIN) or Social Security Number (SSN), W-9 tax certifications, and ACH bank routing credentials.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, device telemetry, browser type, operating system, and on-site interaction patterns.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for transparent business purposes, including:

  • Fulfilling customer orders and coordinating carrier delivery across the United States.
  • Processing multi-vendor transactions, commission reconciliation, and merchant ACH disbursements.
  • Preventing fraudulent transactions and ensuring marketplace trust and platform safety.
  • Complying with federal tax reporting obligations, including IRS Form 1099-K documentation.
  • Communicating order status updates, delivery alerts, and customer service responses.

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) Rights

If you are a resident of California, you are entitled to specific rights regarding your personal information under the California Consumer Privacy Act and the California Privacy Rights Act:

  • Right to Know: You may request disclosure of the specific pieces and categories of personal information we have collected about you over the past 12 months.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request deletion of personal information collected from you, subject to legal and regulatory exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: You may request correction of inaccurate personal information maintained in your profile.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: AMEZA does not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising for monetary consideration.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you, alter pricing, or deny service quality for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise your California privacy rights, submit a verified request by emailing privacy@ameza.com or calling toll-free 1-800-555-0123.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential and functional cookies to preserve shopping cart state, maintain authenticated user sessions, remember currency preferences, and compile anonymized site analytics. You may manage cookie preferences through your web browser settings.

6. Data Security & Retention

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, destruction, or disclosure. All transaction traffic is encrypted with industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption.
